A seaside house near Kolding, Denmark, where artists step away from the pressure to produce and take time to create, reflect and restore.

## A house, a home

Ndako means house or home in Lingala. The name comes from the roots of host and curator Chéria Essieke-Bayer. The residency lives in a seaside villa in Bjert, a family summerhouse for years, now open to artists. Residents work in dialogue with nature and around themes of identity, environment, movement and belonging.

## Locally rooted, internationally connected

Ndako brings together artists from African cultural contexts, artists from Denmark and international artists. Residents meet the cultural ecosystem of Kolding: local artists, workshops and institutions. Through the Ubuntu Festival, supported by the Municipality of Kolding, they can present work and develop projects with the local community.

## Sponsor a seat

Fund a named residency seat for one artist in one cohort. Sponsors receive a public programme moment with the residents and a report on the residency. Sponsors support the programme; they do not steer curation. Foundations, embassies, municipalities and brands are welcome to get in touch.

A monthly membership for people who want to keep the house open. Friends receive studio notes from the residents, invitations to open studios and first access to editions.

## FAQ

**Who can apply to Ndako?**
Artists from African cultural contexts, from Denmark and from international backgrounds. Criteria for the next call will be published with it.

**Does it cost anything to take part?**
No. Artists never pay to take part. The residency is supported by grants, sponsored seats and Friends of Ndako.

**Where is Ndako?**
In a seaside villa in Bjert, near Kolding, Denmark.
