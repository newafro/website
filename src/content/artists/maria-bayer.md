Maria Bayer is an emerging artist, model, and communication design student of Congolese and German background. She has been engaged with art since her childhood in Benin, where she began painting at the age of three.

She has accompanied New Afro for several years, assisting in the curation, design, communication of exhibitions.

In 2024, she presented her first painting, A Postcard from Within, conceived as an open letter to herself.
