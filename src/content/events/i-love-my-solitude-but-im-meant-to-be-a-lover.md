## About the event

In a world where the stigma around mental health persists, Neo's art provides a platform for dialog and understanding. Through her bold and emotional creations, she invites viewers to step into the shoes of those struggling with mental health issues, encouraging empathy and compassion.

Cheria Essieke - the curator - warmly invites all art enthusiasts to immerse themselves in the fascinating world of Neo, a talented artist from Abidjan. Through her thought-provoking artworks, Neo offers an immersive exploration of the complexities of human relationships and mental health. Based on personal experiences and critical observations of society, Neo's vivid and contrasting caricatures serve as a touching reflection of the human condition.
