## About the event

Benjamin Abana is a talented Nigerian-Danish artist, who won in 2019 the title of 'danish best portrait painter' on the Danish national tv show.

Between traditional and modern portrait painting methods, Europe and Africa, happiness and sadness, Benjamin found his signature and it fits him well.

The artist shares the same ideas with New Afro: cultural exchanges between cultures to enable social development.

This is the beginning of a nice and long-term collaboration with Benjamin Abana. Together, we decided to rebuild culturally.
