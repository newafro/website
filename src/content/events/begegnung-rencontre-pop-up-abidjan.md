## About the event

Creativity or art does'nt have any color. Through this event curated by Cheria Essieke (New Afro), we want to connect with Ivorian creatives around some Germans ones. We believe in the strengh of fair, positive cultural exchanges to reduce cultural stereotypes and make the art industry a place where every talented and hard working creative has the chance to export itself. Through this event, we want to be the bidge between two creative world and facilate connections and future creative collaborations.

German creatives: Nilandmon, Charlotte am main, L$D, M'anappelle, Thisisearly, Blingz, Sekt Vaux, Catrice Cosmetics

If You're somewhere around Abidjan, join us at the creative space and concept store: Dozo Abidjan
