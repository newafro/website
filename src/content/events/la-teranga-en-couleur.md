## About the event

Teranga means hospitality in Wolof (the senegalese national language). The Senegalese Flag has tree colors: red, yellow and green. Each of these colors means something deep. When Part Mainz contacted us for a collaboration with the Institut Francais Mainz around the Journée de la francophonie, Senegal came first!

The country of Teranga is rich in culture and never stopped influencing the cultural industry in Africa and somewhere else.

Therefore, We invite you 16.03.2023 at 19:00 Pm to the opening of our exhibition: La Teranga en Couleur at the French Institut Mainz (Germany). Bring friends, Family or your love with you and lets celebrate art around nice talks, artworks and wine. Don't worry, if you miss the opening event, the exposition will be open every day until 30.03.2023 for visitors.

Creatives: Afiaarts, Jean-baptiste Joire, LSD.

Event curated by Cheria (New Afro) in collaboration with Part Mainz and Institut français Mainz.
