## Time & Location

13 Dec 2024, 18:00 - 22:00

Mainz, Am Zollhafen, 55118 Mainz, Germany

## About the event

We are delighted to announce the 2nd edition of our annual private showroom: #Africancreativestotheworld.

Once again, we are showcasing a curated selection of creative, handcrafted treasures from brands within the African diaspora and across the African continent.

You are looking for meaningful, sustainable, and innovative gifts for your loved ones? Join us on 13.12 from 18-22 Pm at our private showroom to celebrate the season of giving. Discover unique pieces that tell inspiring stories and highlight the richness of African cultures and creativity.

As this is a private event, we kindly ask you to register by 10.12 to secure your spot and help us organize better. Please send us an e-mail at: hello@newafro.net (your name and the number of persons). Once registered, you will get an e-mail from us with informations about the location.

We can't wait!
