Our next event will take place this year from 19.05-21.05.2022 in Cologne

Pre-registrations are already possible via our Events Page.

Tickets will be sold out-door 15 Euro daily. This will give you access to an exhibition full of colors and emotions. Every day for 3 days, 15 of Benjamin Abana's portrait paintings will be exposed.

On 19.05.2022 at 3 Pm the New Afro team will welcome you with a glass of Vaux Sekt (great and tasty sparkling wine made in the Rhein-Main-Gebiet Region in Germany), delicious finger food, and nice and chilled Afro-Pop music. We naturally thought about the ones, who don't drink alcohol. We have fresh natural juices for you.

20.05.2022 Benjamin Abana will Live-paint a portrait, that will be directly sold at auction later on that evening. Of course, we promise also for the second day to provide you with a glass of Vaux Sekt, tasty finger food, and Afro music for your soul.

21.05.2022 purchase one or many of Benjamin Abana Portrait Paintings, and get to know more about the artist, and his art philosophy through great conversations. Meet the New Afro team, connect with other art lovers and let culturally build stronger bonds around this exhibition. From 8 Pm until 10 Pm our Dj will make us move and forget (for 2 hours :P) about the 2 hard last years we went through and survived (Corona). He had prepared a great, dancing Mix of Afro music.

We are all looking forward to meeting you in Cologne 1 month from now!
