# New Afro Release Content Requests

This list captures content that should not be invented in code. Keep the public
site polished by hiding or marking incomplete material until these items are
confirmed by the New Afro team.

## Journal / Blog

- Verified article body copy for each imported Wix journal post.
- Hero and gallery image credits for each journal post.
- Decision on which posts should be published at launch. Imported posts are
  currently kept as drafts until copy and credits are confirmed.

## Art.Store

- Decision on whether Art.Store should be visible at launch.
- If yes: product list, artwork titles, artists, images, prices, dimensions,
  edition details, shipping notes, payment flow, and refund terms.
- If no: keep Art.Store references limited to the About narrative and avoid a
  public shop route until the inventory is ready.

## Archive / Projects

- Final titles, dates, venues, curators, artists, and captions for each archive
  project.
- Image credits and usage confirmation for event posters, exhibition photos,
  installation photos, and partner marks.
- Decision on whether each archive item links to a detail page, an external
  event page, or remains a visual timeline entry.

## About / Team

- Confirm team names, roles, email addresses, and preferred public headshots.
- Confirm whether the public About page should include a full team section or
  keep the shorter Wix-style narrative only.

## Forms And Contact

- Contact form delivery target and spam-protection approach.
- Newsletter provider and signup endpoint.
- Until these are wired, keep visible notes telling visitors to email directly.

## Artist Inquiries

- Preferred receiving email: `hello@newafro.net`, `cheria@newafro.net`, or a
  dedicated submissions address.
- Any required submission fields: portfolio, location, discipline, CV, images,
  availability, or collaboration type.

## Legal / Credits

- Final imprint, privacy policy, cookie/privacy position, and image-credit
  format for production.
- Partner logo usage approvals where partner marks appear on public pages.
